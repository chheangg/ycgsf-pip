# Rules & prizes

## Format rules (from MLH event about + emails + challenge pages)

- In-person Hack Day at UC San Diego (DS3-hosted).
- Build with **open-source or open-weight AI** as an important part of the project (for the main open-source track).
- Solo or teams; all experience levels welcome.
- Bring a laptop (and charger).
- Submit through **OrganizerHQ Challenges** on the day.
- **Submissions close: Sun Oct 25, 2026, 6:30 PM PDT.**
- **One submission can enter every category it qualifies for.**
- Registration & check-in managed on Organizer HQ / MyMLH.
